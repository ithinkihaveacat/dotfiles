# Email Guidelines

This document provides guidelines for writing technical outreach, follow-up, and
partner communication emails.

## Core Principles

- **Leave wriggle room on external observations:** When flagging behavior
  observed from outside a team's or partner's codebase (such as black-box
  testing, trace analysis, or inspecting a single build), frame findings with
  humility rather than certainty—acknowledging that your local setup may be
  misconfigured, gated by a server flag, or already addressed in a newer build
  (e.g., *"doesn't seem to show paired devices—is my setup broken?"* or *"we're
  looking at this from the outside on a single build and could be missing a flag
  or in-flight change"*).
- **Hold back full solutions and internal numbers for the conversation:** When
  setting up a meeting or starting a technical thread, hint at how you can help
  directionally (*"Seems like there are a few opportunities here"*, *"we have
  some reference patterns that could make this easier"*) rather than dumping
  every prescriptive code change or exact internal telemetry figure into the
  email. This keeps the email readable, avoids sharing unvetted internal metrics
  in writing, and gives the recipient a clear reason to meet.
